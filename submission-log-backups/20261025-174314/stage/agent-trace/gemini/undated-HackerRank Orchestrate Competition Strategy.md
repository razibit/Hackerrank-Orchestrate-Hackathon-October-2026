### 2. The Challenge Explained Simply

#### Intuitive Overview

Imagine a customer named Leila clicks "Delete My Account" on an e-commerce site (*Northstar Shop*). The company's deletion script deletes her row from the main database and marks her ticket `COMPLETE`. However:

1. A failover database replica (`customer_accounts_failover_replica_20260902.sql`) never synced the deletion and still holds her full name, email, and phone number.
2. An active ETL pipeline (`etl_pipeline_01`) or unblocked backup archive (`backup_vault`) is scheduled to run tonight and will copy her old profile back into the main database—resurrecting her as "ghost data."
3. Meanwhile, another user (Haruka) emails support saying *"My phone number is outdated, please replace it and leave my account active."* The company's buggy ticket router accidentally dumps Haruka's ticket into the deletion queue (`deletion_requests.csv`).

Your job is to build an automated **Deletion Audit Agent** that investigates 518 cases in `dataset/deletion_requests.csv` as of each case's exact `audit_at` timestamp:

- **Step 1 (Intent Check)**: Did the person actually ask to delete their account/personal data? If they only asked to update a phone number, opt out of marketing emails, reset a password, or download an invoice, output `NOT_APPLICABLE` with `[]`.
- **Step 2 (Identity Resolution)**: Find the user in `user_identities.csv` and trace any old emails, phone numbers, names, or email hashes they ever used in `user_audit_log.csv`.
- **Step 3 (Point-in-Time Evidence Audit)**: Search the 94 evidence files (`dataset/evidence/`) captured ** at or before `audit_at`**. Never trust `reported_status` or `system_activity.csv` claims without evidence proof.
- **Step 4 (Policy & Remediation Plan)**: Check every discovered record against the 15 rules in `deletion_rules.csv` (some records must be `DELETE`d, some `REDACT`ed, some `ANONYMIZE`d, and some—like `SECURITY_AUDIT_LOG` and `LEGAL_HOLD_RECORD`—must be `RETAIN`ed!).


    - If everything complies at `audit_at`, output `COMPLETE` and list the `VERIFICATION` steps (citing the `evidence_id` proving compliance).
    - If any record violates policy or an active sync/backup can resurrect data, output `INCOMPLETE` and list:


        1. `FINDING` steps (what is still exposed/active, with `evidence_id`),
        2. `REMEDIATION` steps (how to fix it—always stopping active data flows and blocking restores *before* deleting/redacting/anonymizing records, with `evidence_id: ""`), and
        3. `VERIFICATION` steps (confirming the final state of *all* relevant records for that user, with `evidence_id: ""`).

#### Concrete Walkthrough: Beginner-Friendly vs. Advanced Engineering Level

Take **`case_05`** (`leila.haddad@yahoo.com`, `reported_status=COMPLETE` at `2026-08-28`, `audit_at=2026-09-08T13:40:00Z`):

- **Beginner View**: Leila says *"I deleted my account last week. Please confirm all my data has been purged."* Even though the system claims `COMPLETE`, when we look inside `evidence_54` (`customer_accounts_failover_replica_20260902.sql`, captured Sept 2), `customer_profiles_primary` does not have her, ** but** `customer_profiles_failover_replica` still has row `'account_005','user_05','Leila Haddad','leila.haddad@yahoo.com'`. So her deletion is `INCOMPLETE`! We output 3 steps: (1) `FINDING`: `REPORT_REMAINING_RECORD` on `account_005` citing `evidence_54`; (2) `REMEDIATION`: `DELETE_RECORD` on `account_005` (`evidence_id=""`); (3) `VERIFICATION`: `VERIFY_RECORD_ABSENT` on `account_005` (`evidence_id=""`).
- **Advanced Engineering View**: Now look at **`case_29`** (`noah.f438085@outlook.com`, `audit_at=2026-09-15T12:00:00Z`). In `evidence_92` (`service_resource_export_20260915_114455.json`), Noah's `account_service` record `account_7012` is `404` (`ABSENT`), and his `security_service` record `audit_record_3954` is properly retained (`PRESENT`). However, 4 violations exist in `evidence_92`: `search_1993` is still present, `analytics_5208` still has exposed PII, `support_5281` still has exposed PII, and `integration_service` `data_flow_3827` is still `ACTIVE`.Notice a critical subtlety in the golden answer for `case_29` in [`sample_output.csv`](https://github.com/interviewstreet/hackerrank-orchestrate-october26/blob/main/dataset/sample_output.csv):


    - Phases are ordered `FINDING` (steps 1–4) → `REMEDIATION` (steps 5–8) → `VERIFICATION` (steps 9–14).
    - Inside `REMEDIATION`, `DISABLE_DATA_FLOW` (`step_no: 5`) comes **first** before `DELETE_RECORD` (`step_no: 6`), `REDACT_RECORD` (`step_no: 7`), and `ANONYMIZE_RECORD` (`step_no: 8`).
    - Inside `VERIFICATION` (steps 9–14), **not only** do we verify the 4 remediated resources (`search_1993`, `support_5281`, `analytics_5208`, `data_flow_3827`), ** we also include verification steps for the already-compliant resources** (`account_7012` `VERIFY_RECORD_ABSENT` at step 9, and `audit_record_3954` `VERIFY_RECORD_PRESENT` at step 11), all with `evidence_id: ""`! Missing already-compliant resources in the `VERIFICATION` phase of an `INCOMPLETE` case is the #1 hidden trap in this dataset.

### 6. Recommended Architecture: Typed-Seam Hybrid Agentic Engine

Why does **Architecture C** win both the ** Output CSV** (30%) and ** Code ZIP** (30%) rubrics simultaneously?In [`Behind the Scenes of HackerRank Orchestrate`](https://www.hackerrank.com/blog/behind-the-scenes-of-hackerrank-orchestrate/), the Code ZIP rubric weights ** Agent architecture (30%)** (*"Is this an agent, or a hardcoded workflow with LLM calls? Look for tool-calling loops, model-driven routing, and multi-agent handoffs"*) and ** Prompt and tool craft (30%)** (*"Quality of system prompts and tool descriptions"*). Meanwhile, Swayam Mishra ([3rd in June](https://swayam-mishra.medium.com/how-to-build-ai-agents-you-can-actually-trust-d0c37289f48d)) and Mrinal Desai (#1 in August) proved that letting an LLM write final CSV outputs directly causes fatal output regressions.

**The Winning Synthesis**:

1. **Stage 1 — Multi-Modal & Multilingual Perception Agent (LLM with Tool-Calling & Disk Cache)**:

2. **Wait—when is `account_service` omitted from `VERIFICATION` in an `INCOMPLETE` case?** Look at `case_09` in `sample_output.csv`: `case_09` (`k.appiah88@gmail.com`, `reported_status=NOT_STARTED`) has only 3 steps (`REPORT_RESTORE_RISK` → `BLOCK_RESTORE` → `VERIFY_RESTORE_BLOCKED` on `backup_4978` from `evidence_93`). Because in `evidence_93` (`customer_data_inventory_20260916.sql`), `k.appiah88@gmail.com` ONLY has a row in `backup_restore_entries` (`backup_4978`) and no `account_service` lookup or record in the evidence! Thus, **only emit verification steps for resources that are actually evidenced for that subject at `<= audit_at`**.
3. **`REPORT_REMAINING_RECORD` vs `REPORT_EXPOSED_PII` on `analytics_platform` and `support_platform`**:Look closely at `sample_output.csv`:


    - In `case_07` (`evidence_58`: `delivery_correction_attachment.pdf`, a `SUPPORT_ATTACHMENT` under `rule_06`) and `case_10` (`evidence_52`: `support_002`), the finding is `REPORT_REMAINING_RECORD` and remediation is `DELETE_RECORD`.

Your `code/evaluation/evaluation_report.md` is a **mandatory graded file** inside `code.zip`. The automated code judge checks that it contains:
